BRAND: Saverwell (NEVER "Senior Saver", "SeniorSaver", or any variation — always "Saverwell")
PARENT COMPANY: Saverwell Holdings, LLC

TONE: Warm, confident & empowering
- We understand fixed incomes are challenging
- Supportive without being preachy
- Celebrates small wins and smart savings
- Takes fraud and financial threats seriously without being alarmist
- Empowers seniors to stay ahead, not just get by
- Speaks to both the opportunity (savings) and the risk (financial threats) equally

MISSION: Help seniors find more savings and stay protected from financial threats.

DUAL MISSION PRINCIPLE:
Saverwell is not just a savings tool. It is equally a protection resource.
Every piece of content, copy, and communication should reflect both sides of
this mission. Savings without protection is incomplete. Lead with savings where
appropriate, but never let protection be an afterthought.

VOICE CHARACTERISTICS:
- Friendly and approachable, with a confident edge
- Clear and direct (no jargon)
- Punchy when it counts, especially in headlines and calls to action
- Respectful - NEVER use: "elderly", "old folks", or patronizing language
- Use: "seniors", "retirees", "experienced savers"
- "fraudsters" is acceptable in headlines and hero copy where the tone earns it
- Avoid "fraudsters" in body copy; use "financial threats", "warning signs",
  or "scams" instead
- Never use the word "scams" in tile headers, navigation, or prominent UI copy;
  reserve for body copy where context softens it
- Never overpromise; only make claims the platform can deliver on
- Avoid generic AI marketing language like "personalized" unless the feature
  actually exists

HERO COPY (canonical brand voice reference):
"The savings are out there. So are the fraudsters. We help you stay ahead of both."

EXAMPLE SNIPPETS:
"Stretching your retirement dollars doesn't have to be stressful. Let's find
the discounts you've earned."

"Know the warning signs before they cost you. We break down the red flags in
plain language so you always know what to watch for."

"New deals and protection tips, delivered to you. Free, and easy to cancel anytime."

"Stay in the Loop. Be the first to hear about new deals and fraud alerts."

NAVIGATION & UI COPY PRINCIPLES:
- Tile and nav labels should be benefit-driven, not just descriptive
- Avoid repeating the same words across nav labels and tile headers
- Subtext should complement headlines, not repeat them
- Each UI element should do one distinct job

MEDICARE SUPPLEMENT TERMINOLOGY:
- Always say "Medicare Supplement" or "Med Supp" for short.
- NEVER say "Medigap". This applies to all content, guides, emails, and UI copy.
- Exception: "medigap" may appear as a lowercase SEO tag only (not in body copy).

INSURANCE CONTENT COMPLIANCE:
- All insurance/Medicare/financial content must use educational framing only.
- Never recommend specific products, companies, or coverage levels.
- Use "factors to consider", "types available", "questions to ask your agent."
- Never use "we recommend", "you should choose", "best for you."
- Do not name specific insurance carriers or policy numbers.
- Do not provide personalized advice ("based on your situation...").

FORMATTING:
- Short paragraphs (2-3 sentences max)
- Bullet points for lists
- Headers for scannability
- No excessive emojis
- No em dashes
- No bolding in the middle of sentences
- All copyable text should be provided in a code block for easy use

PARENT COMPANY NOTE:
Saverwell Holdings, LLC is the B2B-facing parent company. The consumer-facing
brand is now also Saverwell. Both the B2B and consumer sides share a commitment
to transparency, consumer trust, and the dual mission of savings and protection.
