# Brand Voice — UnitedHealth Group / Optum
# =========================================
# Covers UHG, Optum, Optum Financial, and Optum Bank as a single workspace.
# Refine the sections below as you gather official brand standards.

BRAND NAME: UnitedHealth Group (parent) — Optum, Optum Financial, Optum Bank (operating units)

MISSION: Help people live healthier lives and help make the health system work better for everyone.

PARENT BRAND CONTEXT:
- UHG is a Fortune 5 diversified health care company.
- Optum is the health services arm (analytics, pharmacy, financial services).
- Optum Financial focuses on HSA, FSA, HRA, and commuter benefits.
- Optum Bank is the federally chartered bank that holds HSA deposits.
- All four brands operate under UHG enterprise governance.

TONE:
- Clinically credible — accuracy first, never overstate
- Plain-language — write for members and providers, not lawyers or executives
- Calm, confident, and direct — health and money decisions deserve clarity
- Empathetic — readers may be facing an illness, a cost surprise, or paperwork stress
- Forward-looking — emphasize what someone can do next, not what went wrong

AUDIENCE:
- Primary: Members of UHG / Optum health plans and HSA accountholders
- Secondary: Employers, brokers, and benefits administrators
- Tertiary: Providers (hospitals, clinics, pharmacies) interacting with Optum systems
- Note: Audiences span healthy adults, seniors on Medicare, families with chronic conditions, and caregivers — adjust complexity by surface.

VOICE CHARACTERISTICS:
- Use active voice
- Spell out acronyms on first use (e.g., "Health Savings Account (HSA)")
- Define every benefits term the first time you use it
- Use "you" and "your" when addressing members directly
- Lead paragraphs with what the reader gets, not what the company does
- Numbers in numerals (not spelled out) for dollar amounts, percentages, dates

WORDS WE USE (preferred):
- "member" (not "customer" or "user" when referring to insured individuals)
- "accountholder" (for HSA/FSA contexts)
- "plan" (not "policy")
- "provider" (not "doctor's office")
- "claim" (not "bill")
- "qualified medical expense" (HSA-specific — defined term)

WORDS WE AVOID:
- "Cheap" → use "affordable" or "lower cost"
- "Old people" → use "older adults" or "Medicare-eligible adults"
- "Free" alone → always qualify ("at no cost to you", "covered in full")
- Marketing superlatives ("best", "#1") without substantiation
- Em dashes (—) and en dashes (–) — use commas, periods, or " - " instead

COMPLIANCE GUARDRAILS:
- Health claims must be substantiated; flag with [VERIFY] any clinical assertion
- HSA/FSA tax statements must include "consult a tax professional" disclaimers
- HIPAA: never use member-identifying information in marketing copy
- Medicare communications: follow CMS Medicare Marketing Guidelines (annual update)
- Banking communications (Optum Bank): comply with Reg E, Reg DD, FDIC disclosure rules

CONTENT FORMATS:
- Member emails: 150-250 words, one clear call to action
- Newsletters: 4-6 short blocks, member benefit + how to act
- Provider communications: heavier on specifics (codes, dates, deadlines)
- Long-form guides: scannable, H2-driven, FAQ at the end
