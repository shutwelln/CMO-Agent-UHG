# UnitedHealth Group — Business Segments Overview

Source: https://www.unitedhealthgroup.com/uhg/businesses.html
Captured: 2026-05-15

## Top-Line

UHG positions itself as "a health care and well-being company" operating through **two complementary divisions** serving **148 million people globally**:

1. **Optum** — care delivery, data/analytics, and pharmacy services
2. **UnitedHealthcare** — health insurance / benefits

---

## OPTUM

> "Delivers care aided by technology and data, empowering people, partners and providers" with guidance and tools for improved health outcomes.

### Optum Health
- Patient-centered care organization
- Delivers "high-quality, fully accountable value-based care" across communities nationwide
- https://www.optum.com/en/about-us/optum-health.html

### Optum Insight
- Partners with payers, providers, governments, and life sciences firms
- Mission: "simplify and enhance clinical, administrative and financial processes" via software-enabled services and analytics
- Focus: advancing value-based care models
- https://business.optum.com/en/insights.html
- **NOTE:** Optum Financial currently rolls up under Optum Insight from an investor-reporting perspective. See `reference/optum_financial_optum_bank_context.md` for carve-out plans.

### Optum Rx
- Pharmacy benefits / drug access
- Mission: facilitate access to "safe and effective prescription medications and therapies at more affordable costs"
- https://www.optumrx.com

---

## UNITEDHEALTHCARE

> "Provides a full range of health benefits, enabling affordable coverage, simplifying the health care experience."

### UnitedHealthcare Employer & Individual
- Employer-sponsored health benefits + individual/family plans
- https://www.uhc.com

### UnitedHealthcare Medicare & Retirement
- ~13.8 million consumers served
- Products: Medicare Advantage, Medicare Part D, Medicare Supplement, group retiree services
- https://www.uhc.com/medicare

### UnitedHealthcare Community & State
- 7.5 million people served
- Serves economically disadvantaged and medically underserved populations without employer-funded coverage
- https://www.uhc.com/communityplan
