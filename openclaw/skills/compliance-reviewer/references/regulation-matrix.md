# Regulation Checklists & Disclosure Templates

## FINRA Rule 2210 (Communications with the Public)

- [ ] No promissory language ("guaranteed returns", "risk-free", "safe investment")
- [ ] Fair and balanced presentation (risks alongside benefits)
- [ ] No projections of future performance
- [ ] Required disclosures present and prominent
- [ ] No misleading testimonials or endorsements
- [ ] Supervised approval documented

## SEC Rule 206(4)-1 (Investment Adviser Advertising)

- [ ] No misleading performance claims
- [ ] Testimonial rules followed (if applicable)
- [ ] Anti-fraud provisions satisfied
- [ ] Form ADV requirements met

## TCPA (Telephone Consumer Protection Act)

- [ ] Prior express written consent obtained (for marketing SMS)
- [ ] SMS opt-in documented with clear terms
- [ ] STOP/opt-out mechanism present and working
- [ ] Do-Not-Call list compliance
- [ ] No autodialer to cell phones without consent
- [ ] Time restrictions honored (8 AM - 9 PM recipient time)

## CAN-SPAM Act

- [ ] Accurate header information (From, Reply-To)
- [ ] Non-deceptive subject line
- [ ] Message identified as advertisement (if applicable)
- [ ] Physical postal address included
- [ ] Opt-out mechanism present and honored within 10 business days
- [ ] No harvested email addresses

## GDPR (General Data Protection Regulation)

- [ ] Lawful basis established (consent, legitimate interest, contract)
- [ ] Explicit consent obtained (not pre-checked boxes)
- [ ] Right to erasure process in place
- [ ] Right to access/portability supported
- [ ] Data minimization (only necessary data collected)
- [ ] Cross-border transfer mechanisms (if applicable)
- [ ] Privacy notice clear and accessible

## CCPA/CPRA (California Consumer Privacy Act)

- [ ] Right to know what data is collected
- [ ] Right to delete personal information
- [ ] Right to opt-out of sale/sharing
- [ ] "Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information" link present
- [ ] Financial incentive disclosure (if offering rewards for data)
- [ ] Sensitive personal information handling documented

## Disclosure Templates

### Financial Services Email Footer
```
[Company Name] | [Address]
Securities offered through [Broker-Dealer], Member FINRA/SIPC.
Advisory services offered through [RIA], an SEC-registered investment adviser.
Past performance is not indicative of future results. Investing involves risk,
including possible loss of principal.

Unsubscribe: [link] | Privacy Policy: [link]
```

### Insurance Email Footer
```
[Company Name] | [Address] | [License #]
Insurance products are not FDIC insured, not bank guaranteed,
and may lose value. Coverage subject to terms, conditions, and
availability. Not available in all states.

Unsubscribe: [link] | Privacy Policy: [link]
```

[Frequency] msgs/month. Terms: [link]
```

```
